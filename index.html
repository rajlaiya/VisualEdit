[ CLARIFICATIONS ]

Frequently Asked Questions

Pricing structures, turnaround timelines, and project workflow expectations.

What are the rates for short-form reel edits?

Standard basic reel edits start at ₹250 for up to 60 seconds and ₹400 for up to 90 seconds. For recurring content packages or multi-clip monthly retainers, custom volume discounts are available.

What is the standard delivery turnaround?

Standard delivery is 24 to 48 hours from receiving your raw footage and brief. Express same-day delivery is available upon prior request.

What document & graphic design services do you offer?

We design event key art, social campaign posters, PDF formatting, cover letters, ATS-optimized resume redesigns, and executive pitch decks. Note: Unethical or government ID documents are strictly not edited.

How are revisions handled and what file formats will I receive?

Every commission includes 2 rounds of revisions. High-resolution masters are delivered via Google Drive in 4K/1080p MP4, PNG, PDF, or presentation formats.
