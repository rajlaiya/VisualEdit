Frequently Asked Questions

Transparent pricing, turnaround times, and delivery workflows.

What are the rates for 60-second and 90-second reel edits?

Standard rates start at ₹250 for up to 60 seconds and ₹400 for up to 90 seconds. For bulk reel orders or monthly recurring retainers, custom discounted rates are available.

What is the standard delivery time? Is rush delivery available?

Standard delivery is 24 to 48 hours. Urgent same-day rush delivery is available on request depending on project complexity.

What document editing and design services do you provide?

We handle PDF organizing, merging, formatting, ATS-friendly resume redesigns, cover letters, and presentation slide decks. Note: We do not edit government IDs, identity proofs, or unethical documents.

How many revisions are included and how will I receive the final files?

Every project includes 2 free revision rounds to ensure the outcome matches your exact vision. Final high-resolution files are shared via Google Drive or direct WhatsApp links in MP4, PDF, PNG, or PPT formats.
